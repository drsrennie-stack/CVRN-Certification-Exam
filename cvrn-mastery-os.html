The pace assumes first-pass learning plus spaced review. Roughly 65 percent of your minutes go to new competencies and 35 percent to review, because content you learned in week one is worth nothing on exam day if it is not retrieved again before then.

Gap finder

Two passes. First you rate yourself across every domain on the blueprint, which takes about three minutes. Then the system builds a short diagnostic from the domains you rated lowest and from the heaviest-weighted domains regardless of your rating, because self-assessment is reliable about what you know you do not know and unreliable about the rest.

Practice exams

Ten forms, each assembled proportional to the ABCM CVRN-BC blueprint from a pool reserved for exams only. No item appears in the practice or recall decks, and no item repeats across forms, so each score is an independent estimate. A full-length form is the honest estimate; the 30-item quick check is for a mid-week read.
